Methodology & Data Integrity ZERO SYNTHETIC DATA

All figures above represent real users who registered on the Tawfeer platform and confirmed they were going to visit a government service centre before using this digital platform.

Distance calculation: Haversine formula between user's confirmed area coordinates and nearest government service centre, with road multiplier (city trips <30km: ×1.4 · highway trips >30km: ×1.15). Round-trip distance used for all calculations.

CO₂ formula: Distance (km) × 192g/km ÷ 1000 = kg CO₂ (UAE Ministry of Climate Change and Environment passenger vehicle baseline, 2024)

Fuel formula: Distance (km) × 0.08 L/km (Dubai Municipality light vehicle average consumption)

Financial saving: Fuel (L) × AED 2.89/L (UAE Special 95 official pump price, July 2026)
